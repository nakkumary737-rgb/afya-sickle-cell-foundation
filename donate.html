💪 Where Your Gift Goes

Every shilling is tracked. Every impact is real. Here's exactly how we use your trust.

  • 1
    Early Screening & Detection Helps families identify needs before they become emergencies, giving children a real chance at a normal childhood.
  • 2
    Clinical Guidance & Monitoring Routine checkups, prevention plans, and crisis support that keep families confident and children healthy.
  • 3
    Hydroxyurea & Treatment Access Affordable medicine that reduces pain crises by up to 50% and helps kids stay in school and playing with friends.
  • 4
    Family Education & Community Practical tools, emotional support, and peer networks that make families feel less alone and more powerful.
