Clinical Care & Programs

Professional medical monitoring, school outreach, family support, and practical care for every sickle cell warrior.

Program 01 • Clinical Care

Routine medical monitoring

In partnership with Afya Medicare Clinic, we help families manage sickle cell care through proactive monitoring and practical guidance that reduce crisis frequency and improve long-term health outcomes.

  • Routine blood reviews and hemoglobin checks
  • Personalized hydration and crisis prevention plans
  • Essential medication and treatment guidance
  • Early management of complications and follow-up support
Program 02 • Awareness & Screening

Genotype screening & school advocacy

AFYA helps communities understand sickle cell early through school-based outreach, genotype screening, and education that reduces fear, stigma, and missed opportunities for early intervention.

  • On-site genotype testing drives in schools and communities
  • Awareness workshops for parents, teachers, and youth
  • Early child screening and timely care pathways
  • Education that prevents stigma and misinformation
Program 03 • Community & Hope

Parent & warrior support circles

Caring for a child with sickle cell can feel heavy. Our support circles build community, reduce isolation, and connect families to practical resources and emotional encouragement.

  • Peer-to-peer parent networks and shared experience
  • Mental wellness and emotional support guidance
  • Nutrition and home-care recommendations
  • Emergency preparedness and day-to-day coping tools
