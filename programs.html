Our Programs & Impact

Comprehensive medical support, early detection, and compassionate community care designed for sickle cell warriors and their families.

How We Support Our Warriors

Through clinical collaboration and community-driven action, our programs address both medical and emotional needs at every stage.

Education & Outreach

Sickle Cell Public Awareness

Dismantling myths, fighting social stigma, and conducting educational workshops in schools, places of worship, and local communities to promote understanding and empathy.

Clinical Partnership

Routine Clinical Care & Reviews

In partnership with Afya Medicare, registered members receive organized clinical check-ups, regular lab monitoring, routine medication reviews, and ongoing health tracking.

Early Detection

Genotype Screening Drives

Facilitating accessible genotype testing for young adults, intending couples, and newborns to encourage early detection, informed family planning, and timely medical care.

Crisis Response

Pain Crisis Management Support

Equipping families with home-care guidelines, hydration regimens, and rapid-response protocols to effectively manage pain crises and know when emergency clinical intervention is needed.

Psychosocial Support

Parent & Caregiver Support Circles

Providing safe spaces for parents—inspired by the founders' journey—to share challenges, alleviate emotional isolation, and learn practical care strategies from one another.

Holistic Wellness

Nutritional & Lifestyle Guidance

Educating families on optimal dietary habits, proper hydration habits, and lifestyle modifications that strengthen immunity and reduce crisis triggers for warriors.

"Through education we prevent, through care we sustain, and through love we restore hope."
