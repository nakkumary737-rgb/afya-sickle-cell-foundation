Clinical Care & Programs

Combining professional medical monitoring with community outreach to support every warrior in Uganda.

PROGRAM 01 • CLINICAL CARE

Routine Medical Monitoring

In partnership with Afya Medicare Clinic, we ensure that sickle cell warriors receive proactive, preventive healthcare to reduce the frequency and severity of painful vaso-occlusive crises.

  • Comprehensive routine blood reviews and hemoglobin checks
  • Personalized crisis prevention and hydration planning
  • Accessible essential medication guidance and consultations
  • Early diagnosis and management of sickle cell complications
PROGRAM 02 • ADVOCACY & OUTREACH

Genotype Screening & School Advocacy

Knowledge is the ultimate key to prevention and compassion. We conduct community sensitization drives and genotype testing in schools across Uganda to dismantle social stigma and encourage early detection.

  • On-site genotype testing drives in schools and communities
  • Educational workshops for teachers, parents, and students
  • Awareness campaigns to eradicate myth and social isolation
  • Early child screening to support timely medical intervention
PROGRAM 03 • COMMUNITY & HOPE

Parent & Warrior Support Circles

Caring for a child with sickle cell can feel overwhelming for parents. Our support circles offer a safe space for families to connect, share experiences, and receive practical guidance on home crisis management.

  • Peer-to-peer parent support networks and practical tips
  • Mental health guidance and emotional solidarity
  • Nutritional advice for warrior wellness and strength
  • Emergency preparation and home care training
