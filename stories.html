Brian’s journey from frequent admissions to top of class

📍 Wakiso District, Uganda

For years, Brian missed much of each school term due to sudden, severe painful crises. His mother spent nights worrying and hoping for a better rhythm.

When he joined the AFYA support network, his routine reviews, hydration guidance, and family education changed everything. He is now more stable, stronger, and reclaiming his place in school.

“Before AFYA, I thought I would never finish primary school. Now I want to become a doctor and help other warriors.” — Brian

Grace’s voice: overcoming stigma and finding strength

📍 Mukono District, Uganda

Grace was overwhelmed after learning her twin daughters had sickle cell disease. Social stigma and isolation made each day heavier.

Joining AFYA’s parent support circle helped her rediscover confidence and courage. Today, she is a vocal advocate for understanding, care, and dignity for families navigating similar experiences.

“AFYA gave my daughters care—and gave me my confidence back as a mother.” — Grace
19 Years Old • Youth Ambassador
Living beyond boundaries

Emmanuel: championing awareness in schools

📍 Kampala, Uganda

Growing up, Emmanuel hid his diagnosis from classmates for fear of being mocked or excluded. The emotional burden was heavy.

Through AFYA’s advocacy and peer support, he embraced his story and began speaking in schools, helping young people understand sickle cell with compassion instead of fear.

“Sickle cell is part of my journey, but it is not my destiny.” — Emmanuel
