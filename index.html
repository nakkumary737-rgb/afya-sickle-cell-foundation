Who We Are

Vision • Mission • Values

We believe sickle cell doesn't have to mean the end of dreams. It means we show up differently.

🎯

Our Vision

A Uganda where every child living with sickle cell has access to quality clinical care, lives with dignity, stays in school, and never stops dreaming.

❤️

Our Mission

We provide routine medical support, early screening, family education, and community care that reduces crisis frequency and helps families thrive together.

🤝

Our Values

Compassion without judgment. Care that's practical and affordable. Community that lifts families up. Every child matters. Every life is precious.

Our Story

Rooted in love, shaped by resilience.

AFYA began with a mother's love and a child's courage. What started as one family's search for answers became a movement of hope for many families living with sickle cell disease.

Every painful crisis, every missed school day, every sleepless night reminded us that care must be more than treatment—it must be understanding, dignity, and support that reaches the heart of the family.

Today, AFYA exists to make sure no child feels alone, no parent feels helpless, and no dream is delayed by fear or lack of access.

"Our mission is simple: protect childhood, restore hope, and help every warrior keep living with strength."
