WHY AFYA EXISTS

We believe no one should face sickle cell alone.

Sickle cell disease can affect childhood, education, work, relationships and family life. Yet many of its challenges become easier to face when people have the right information, timely care and a supportive community.

AFYA Sickle Cell Foundation exists to bring those pieces together — through education, advocacy, practical support and a commitment to better sickle cell care.
