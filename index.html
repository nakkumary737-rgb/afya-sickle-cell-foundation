FOUNDED ON FAMILY & EMPATHY

Turning Personal Pain into a Beacon for Thousands

When founders Kittengo Alysious and Nanyonjo Noeline discovered their daughter was living with sickle cell disease, they experienced the fear, uncertainty, and social isolation firsthand.

Determined to change the narrative in Uganda, they established the Afya Sickle Cell Foundation. By uniting parental love with clinical expertise through Afya Medicare, the foundation provides routine medical monitoring, school testing drives, and emotional solidarity for families.
