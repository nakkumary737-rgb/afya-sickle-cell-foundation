WHY WE EXIST

Behind every diagnosis is a person.

A child who wants to play. A student who wants to learn. A mother who wants to see her child grow. A young person with plans, dreams and a future worth fighting for.

AFYA exists because people living with sickle cell deserve more than medical information. They deserve understanding, dignity, support and hope.
