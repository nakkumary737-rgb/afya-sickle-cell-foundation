Born From Personal Strength

Turning Pain into a Light for Thousands

When founders Kittengo Alysious and Nanyonjo Noeline learned their firstborn daughter was living with sickle cell disease, they experienced the denial, emotional weight, and medical strain firsthand.

They built the Afya Sickle Cell Foundation to ensure no parent walks that path alone. Attached directly to Afya Medicare, we combine heartfelt parental compassion with medical reviews, routine care, and public screening drives.
