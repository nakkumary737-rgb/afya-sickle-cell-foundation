Born From Personal Strength

Turning Pain into a Light for Thousands

When founders Kittengo Alysious and Nanyonjo Noeline learned their daughter was living with sickle cell disease, they experienced the fear and emotional weight firsthand.

They built the Afya Sickle Cell Foundation to ensure no family walks that path alone. Directly connected to Afya Medicare, we combine parental care with routine medical check-ups and community screening.
