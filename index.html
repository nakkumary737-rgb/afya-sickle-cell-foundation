Born From Personal Strength

Turning Pain into a Light for Thousands

When founders Kittengo Alysious and Nanyonjo Noeline learned their daughter was living with sickle cell disease, they experienced the confusion, fear, and emotional weight firsthand.

They established the Afya Sickle Cell Foundation to ensure no parent walks that path alone. Directly tied to Afya Medicare, we combine parental empathy with medical reviews, routine care, and screening drives across Uganda.
