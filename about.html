Our Journey & Purpose

Dedicated to creating a world where every individual with sickle cell disease lives with dignity, hope, and optimal care.

Our Mission

To empower sickle cell warriors and their families through holistic support, advocacy, early education, and community solidarity, ensuring no warrior faces pain or stigma in isolation.

Our Vision

A compassionate society free from sickle cell stigma, where early diagnosis, equitable healthcare, and lifelong support enable every warrior to thrive.

Why We Exist

Born Out of Empathy & Strength

Afya Sickle Cell Foundation was established to bridge the gap between medical management and emotional resilience. Sickle cell disease presents unique physical, emotional, and financial challenges that require a united community response.

Through our educational initiatives, support circles, and public awareness campaigns, we aim to transform how society understands sickle cell disease while standing firmly alongside warriors at every step of their journey.

Our Core Values

Compassion

Putting care, empathy, and respect at the center of every community interaction.

Resilience

Honoring the strength of warriors and helping families rise above challenges.

Advocacy

Speaking up for equal healthcare access, awareness, and dignity for all warriors.
