Our Story & Founders

From one family's personal journey to a nationwide foundation of clinical care, advocacy, and hope across Uganda.

7 Years Old • Primary Two (P.2)
OUR GUIDING LIGHT

Anabel Esther Kittengo

📍 Seeds of Gold Primary School

Every powerful movement is born from a quiet, deeply personal moment. For the AFYA Sickle Cell Foundation, that spark is Anabel Esther Kittengo.

Diagnosed with sickle cell disease at a young age, Anabel showed extraordinary strength through early hospital visits and painful crises. Today, as a joyful 7-year-old student in Primary Two at Seeds of Gold Primary School, her smile and determination light up every room she enters.

Anabel represents every child living with sickle cell in Uganda—showing that with early detection, continuous clinical monitoring, and overwhelming family support, a diagnosis is never the end of a child's story.

"Anabel taught us that fighting sickle cell isn't just about managing pain—it's about protecting the childhood, dreams, and future of every warrior."
THE FOUNDERS

Kittengo Alysious & Nanyonjo Noeline

When founders Kittengo Alysious and Nanyonjo Noeline first received Anabel’s diagnosis, they faced the fear, medical uncertainty, and social stigma that affect countless families across Uganda.

Driven by unconditional love for their daughter, they chose to turn personal adversity into a life-saving mission. They founded the AFYA Sickle Cell Foundation to ensure no parent walks this path alone.

By establishing an official medical partnership with Afya Medicare Clinic, they built a ecosystem of routine doctor reviews, genotype screening drives, and parent support circles that serve warriors across the nation.
