OUR GUIDING LIGHT

Anabel Esther

📍 Seeds of Gold Primary School

Every powerful movement is born from a quiet, deeply personal moment. For the AFYA Sickle Cell Foundation, that spark is Anabel Esther.

Diagnosed with sickle cell disease at a young age, Anabel showed extraordinary strength through early hospital visits and painful crises. Today, as a joyful 7-year-old pupil, she continues to inspire her family and community with resilience, courage, and a remarkable sense of hope.

Anabel represents every child living with sickle cell in Uganda—showing that with early detection, continuous clinical monitoring, and overwhelming family support, a diagnosis does not define the future.

"Anabel taught us that fighting sickle cell isn't just about managing pain—it's about protecting the childhood, dreams, and future of every warrior."

FAMILY LOVE & SUPPORT

Kittengo Aloysius

Father of Anabel Esther • Husband of Nanyonjo Noeline

Kittengo Aloysius is the father of Anabel Esther and the loving husband of Nanyonjo Noeline. He is a deeply committed family man whose strength and steady presence continue to anchor the family through every challenge associated with sickle cell disease.

His role in the AFYA journey is deeply significant. Together with Nanyonjo, he has been part of the journey that transformed fear into action, pain into purpose, and uncertainty into a ministry of hope for affected children and families.

  • Provides strong emotional support to his daughter and wife during medical challenges.
  • Represents the family strength behind AFYA's mission and vision.
  • Helps build a home rooted in love, resilience, and commitment to care.
THE FOUNDER

Nanyonjo Noeline

When founder Nanyonjo Noeline first received Anabel's diagnosis, she faced the fear, medical uncertainty, and social stigma that confronts countless families affected by sickle cell disease.

Driven by unconditional love for her daughter, she chose to turn personal adversity into a life-saving mission. She founded the AFYA Sickle Cell Foundation to create awareness, improve health literacy, and support families through access to care, early diagnosis, and ongoing encouragement.

By establishing an official medical partnership with Afya Medicare Clinic, she built an ecosystem of routine doctor reviews, genotype screening drives, emotional support, and health education for sickle cell warriors and their caregivers.
