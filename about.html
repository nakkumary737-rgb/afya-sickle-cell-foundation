Our Journey & Purpose

Born from a family's pain, driven by love, and dedicated to empowering every sickle cell warrior.

Our Mission

To empower sickle cell warriors and their families through holistic support, routine medical guidance, early education, and unconditional love—ensuring no family faces pain or stigma in isolation.

Our Vision

A compassionate society free from sickle cell stigma, where early diagnosis, accessible medical care, and lifelong community support enable every warrior to thrive.

How It All Began

From Denial to Unbreakable Purpose

When Kittengo Alysious and Nanyonjo Noeline held their first daughter in their arms, they envisioned a life full of health and boundless joy. However, as time passed, their world was shaken when she was diagnosed with sickle cell disease.

Like many parents facing this reality for the first time, they initially lived in painful denial. Watching their precious child endure agonizing pain crises—while carrying the overwhelming financial and emotional strain of continuous care—left an indelible mark on their hearts.

Instead of letting despair break them, they turned their agony into advocacy. Driven by fierce parental love and empathy for thousands of families suffering in silence, Alysious and Noeline founded the Afya Sickle Cell Foundation—a beacon of hope dedicated to spreading awareness, easing medical costs, and pouring love into every warrior's life.

Clinical Partnership

Affiliated with Afya Medicare

To ensure our foundation offers actionable, lifesaving impact beyond emotional support, Afya Sickle Cell Foundation is directly attached to Afya Medicare.

Through this partnership, registered foundation members gain direct access to structured medical care designed to keep warriors healthy and out of emergency wards.

  • ✓ Sickle Cell Education: Guiding families on management, nutrition, and crisis prevention.
  • ✓ Routine Care & Reviews: Medical consultations, routine lab check-ups, and specialized tracking.
  • ✓ Holistic Support: Access to emergency guidance, emotional counseling, and family networks.
